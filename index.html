This is the real pitch

Forsyth Park isn’t a private facility. It’s a public park with all the charm that comes with it — uneven ground, dog walkers, and a surface that has never seen a groundskeeper’s love.

The grass is more “tosic class” than Premier League. The mud is real. The dog poo is unavoidable. And somehow the 5-a-side still happens every week.

If you’re looking for perfect lines and soft turf, this isn’t it. If you want competitive games in real conditions with zero pretence, you’re in the right place.

The Standard Ritual

  1. Gather the weapons — 30 tennis balls, 5 handballs, 4 frisbees and the toy rocket all go into one pile in the middle of the playing area.
  2. Frisbees become shields — each person grabs a frisbee and holds it like a shield.
  3. Pegging begins — tennis balls start flying. The goal is simple: hit the other people while using your frisbee to block incoming shots.
  4. Handballs and the rocket enter the mix when things escalate. Accuracy drops, volume rises.
  5. Eventually someone calls time and the actual 5-a-side football begins… or continues in the same spirit.

Frisbee Shields

The only real protection once the tennis balls start flying. Hold it properly or take one in the ribs. Also used for long throws across the park.

Essential Gear

Handball Mix-Ins

Five handballs in the arsenal. Used for heavier throws and when tennis balls alone aren’t enough chaos.

Escalation

Ragebaiting Tosic

Targeted banter directed at one regular. Perfectly timed comments designed to get a reaction. It’s become a recognised part of the session culture.

Local Tradition

Skipping Rope Whips

Skipping ropes used for warm-ups, playful whips, and general chaos. Controlled and between willing participants only.

Warm-up / Fun

Tie Monk with Skipping Ropes

When the energy peaks, skipping ropes get used for more than whipping — someone ends up lightly tied (monk-style). All in good fun and only with consent.

Advanced Chaos

Walking Laps

A few slow laps after a heavy game or a bad loss. Cooling down while navigating the same mud and dog poo you just played on.

Recovery

Kicking Balls into the Forest

Powerful shots and deliberate efforts regularly send footballs deep into the surrounding trees and forest edge. The designated retriever then has to go on a proper expedition while the rest keep playing.

Inevitable Quest

Ragebaiting Tosic

One of the more consistent features of a Forsyth session. Light psychological warfare aimed at a regular player. The comments are usually sharp, the reactions are part of the entertainment, and it somehow never quite dies out.
