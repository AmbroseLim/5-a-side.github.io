The Arsenal Game Ritual

  1. Dump the weapons — 30 tennis balls, 5 handballs, 4 frisbees and the toy rocket into one pile.
  2. Frisbees become shields — grab one. Your dignity depends on it.
  3. Pegging begins — tennis balls fly. Hit people. Block people.
  4. Escalate — handballs and the rocket when things get too calm.
  5. Then 5-a-side, whipping monk, or a trip to the forest.

Essential Gear

Skipping Rope Whips

Warm-ups that turn into playful (and slightly terrifying) whip challenges. Controlled chaos.

Warm-up / Fun

Whipping Monk

Skipping ropes used for ceremonial whipping of the designated monk. Consent required. Dignity optional.

Advanced Chaos

Tie Monk to a Pole

When energy peaks, the monk gets lightly restrained to a pole (or the nearest light post) with the rope. Escape encouraged.

Advanced Chaos

Cowboy Rope Style

Skipping ropes and lassos used with full cowboy energy. Yeehaw optional. Accuracy optional.

Style Points

Rope Arsenal

Extra rope for tying, whipping, or dramatic cowboy moments when the skipping ropes aren’t enough.

Gear

Kicking Balls into the Forest

Powerful or deliberate shots send balls deep into the bush. Someone goes on a full retrieval mission.

Inevitable Quest

Walking in Dog Shit

Core skill and occasional punishment. Navigate the 30% dog-poo surface on a lap of shame.

Survival

Walking Laps of Shame

After a loss or a ragebait gone wrong — slow laps. Reflect. Step carefully. Grow.

Recovery

Throwing Frisbees to the Next Field

Maximum-effort throws aimed at the neighbouring field. Distance over accuracy. Someone has to go get it later.

Long Range

Shooting Soccer Balls Really Hard

Full-power shots with zero regard for the consequences. Often the direct cause of the forest retrieval quest.

Power

Ragebaiting Tosic

The signature tradition. Perfectly timed comments designed to get under one player’s skin. Reactions are legendary. Tosic usually recovers… eventually.
